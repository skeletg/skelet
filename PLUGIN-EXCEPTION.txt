WHAT THIS IS FOR

Skelet is licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3 (see LICENSE).
Skelet is also designed to be extended, and extensions are written against its
interfaces and compiled or deployed alongside it. Without the permission below,
an extension could be treated as a derivative work of Skelet and be forced to
adopt the GPL.

That is not the intent. Extensions belong to the people who write them. A
merchant's private customisation, an agency's client work, and a commercial
plugin sold to third parties are all legitimate, and none of them are required
to publish anything.

The GPL still applies in full to Skelet itself. Modify Skelet's own source and
convey it - ship it, sell it, hand it to a client, publish a rebranded fork -
and the GPL's terms govern that, as intended.

WHAT COUNTS AS AN EXTENSION

"Extension" means software that extends or integrates with Skelet without
modifying Skelet's own source, including:

  - Tier 1 plugins: Go packages registered through the Skelet SDK
    (github.com/skeletg/skelet/sdk) and compiled into the Skelet binary.

  - Remote providers: services implementing the published provider contract and
    called over the network.

  - Storefronts and any other client of Skelet's HTTP or GraphQL APIs, whatever
    language they are written in.

  - Dashboard extensions, admin-UI language packs, CMS block types, themes, and
    email templates.

  - Configuration, migrations, and assets belonging to any of the above.

Importing, subclassing, or implementing an interface declared by the Skelet SDK
does not by itself make an Extension a derivative work of Skelet for the purposes
of this exception.


WHAT IS NOT AN EXTENSION

Changes to Skelet's own source - anything under this repository outside an
Extension's own files - are modifications of Skelet, not Extensions. Renaming
Skelet, re-branding it, forking it, or carving pieces out of it produces a
modified work governed by the GPL, no matter what it is called afterwards.

Bundling Skelet with an Extension does not relicense Skelet. The two keep their
own terms.

You may remove this additional permission from any copy of Skelet you convey, as

copy to the GPL, so there is rarely a reason to.
