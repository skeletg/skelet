- **Saving a draft campaign keeps its own email.** A campaign that carried its own content instead of a library
  email lost it on the next save.
- **A/B results show on the campaign.** A campaign with a subject test lists both subjects' sends, opens and
  clicks, and the winner, once it's scheduled or sent. The table existed, but no page reached it.
- **Saving or scheduling a new campaign no longer asks whether to discard unsaved changes.** The page moved to the
  campaign's own address right after the save, and the leave-page prompt took that for walking away from it.
- **A campaign page says when something failed.** A preview that didn't render, an audience that couldn't be
  counted and a send that couldn't be cancelled each show the reason instead of waiting forever or saying nothing.
