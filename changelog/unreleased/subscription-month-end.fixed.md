- **A monthly subscription started on the 29th-31st no longer skips a month.** Each renewal date was the previous one
  plus a month, so the first short month knocked it off its day for good - started on Jan 31, it next billed on Mar 3,
  with no February invoice, and stayed on the 3rd. Renewals now count from the subscription's start (or the end of its
  trial, or when it was resumed), and a month too short for the day bills on its last day instead: Jan 31, Feb 28,
  Mar 31. Yearly plans started on Feb 29 bill on Feb 28 in other years. Existing subscriptions keep the date they're
  on now.
