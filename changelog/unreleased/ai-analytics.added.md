- **AI analytics in Signals.** **AI insights** under Agentic Commerce compares shoppers who chatted with the assistant
  against those who didn't (conversion, average order, return rate), lists the products it shows with how many got
  carted or ordered after, and counts shoppers' own AI agents by name. **Lens, Search** adds what shoppers asked the
  assistant or their agents for that came back empty, and how the AI overview did per search. Segments offer
  *Chatted with the shopping assistant* and *Ordered with AI help*, and a flow on *Abandoned cart the assistant
  filled* takes those carts in place of the usual recovery mail.
