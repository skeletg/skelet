- **The CMS page editor is down to five sections: Layout, Content, SEO, Page settings and Channels.**
  Publishing, scheduling, review and the cache rebuild live in one menu in the header, which also
  says where the page is live. Version history and comments open in their own dialogs, and the
  history says what each version changed and what restoring one would undo. The SEO previews and settings fold into one line that sums up the
  on-page checks, and each block keeps its tracking and visibility settings under Advanced. Blocks
  open and close without jumping, and the one you click stays on screen while another closes. In
  global scope, one notice explains why the layout and content are read-only and offers a scope
  that can edit them. The live preview shrinks to leave the editor room, and on a screen too narrow
  for both it folds to a rail that opens over the editor - it used to squeeze the editor out of
  sight. It can be hidden on wide screens too.
