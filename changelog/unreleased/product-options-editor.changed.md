- **Product options are edited in one dialog.** Each option opens a wide editor with its name,
  how customers answer (picked from described cards), its choices or charge, and when it shows -
  instead of separate inline forms for the option and each value. The list reads as a summary:
  choices with their charges, character and file limits, and conditions in words ("Only when
  Wrapping is Gift"). Charges are typed in money ("3.50 USD", per-store amounts in each store's
  currency) rather than cents, file limits in MB, options reorder with arrows instead of sort
  numbers, and a choice can be linked to a product - or several products added as choices at
  once - through the shared product picker. A staged delete stays visible with an Undo.
