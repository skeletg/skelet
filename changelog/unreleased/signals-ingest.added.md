- **Signals holds browser events to a tracking plan.** Properties a browser may not send (bot flags,
  revenue, currency, an event's own name or page) are dropped, as are unplanned properties, wrong
  types and unknown `$` events. Each is counted under a new **Data health** section on the Lens
  overview. The plan is published in `docs/tracking-plan.md`, generated by `skelet gen`.
