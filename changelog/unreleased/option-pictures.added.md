- **Options and their choices can have pictures.** In the option editor, each choice gets a small picture slot and the
  option one of its own - pick from the media library or upload. On the product page, an option whose choices have
  pictures shows them as tiles to pick from, and an option's own picture sits under its name, like where an engraving
  goes. Pictures load with the option rules in a query of their own, so the main product query stays under its
  complexity cap; deleting the picture from the media library just removes it from the option.
