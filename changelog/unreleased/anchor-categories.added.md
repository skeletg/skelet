- **A parent category's page shows its subcategories' products.** A category is now an anchor by
  default: its storefront listing includes the products of every category beneath it that the
  channel shows, so "Apparel" lists what's in "Shirts" and "Jackets" without assigning each product
  to it again. A subcategory the channel hides hides its branch, and "Show products from
  subcategories" on the category page switches it off. A remote search engine receives the tree as
  `categoryIds`, and the category CSV carries it as `is_anchor`.
