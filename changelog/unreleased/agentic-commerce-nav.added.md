- **Agentic Commerce in the dashboard.** A new sidebar section for how AI agents meet the store. **Overview**
  opens it: what the shopping assistant answered, couldn't answer and sold and what shoppers' agents ordered, day
  by day over the last 7, 30 or 90 days, and a list of what's switched on for the store - provider, assistant, its
  voice, kept conversations, shoppers' agents, search by meaning and cost prices - each saying what it still needs. **MCP** shows
  both endpoints for the selected store: the address shoppers' agents use, whether they can use it right now
  and why not, and the address and command your team's agents connect with, plus every tool each side can
  call. **Conversations** (with Signals on) reads the conversations the store keeps, and filters to the ones with a message the store
  couldn't answer - nothing said, cut short, every search empty, or the assistant saying the store's information
  didn't cover it - with a count for each, opening a conversation at that message. **Hybrid search**'s
  Assistant tab runs the assistant's two searches for a question you type, on the store and in the language you pick: what the words matched, the nearest by meaning
  with their distance and whether they count, what the model is then shown in its order, and the tools' answers word
  for word - above it, how much of the store is embedded with the current model. **Assistant voice** sets
  what the assistant is called (shown as its panel's title), how it sounds - each tone with an example reply,
  and a preview of the panel as you change them - and up to 4,000 characters of the store's own instructions, staged behind the page's Save like any settings page. Settings can now
  carry a character cap, which the API enforces and every settings field counts down to.
