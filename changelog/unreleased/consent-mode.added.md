- **Shops that don't need a cookie banner can switch it off.** Settings → General → Privacy →
  Cookie consent, per shop. "Ask first" is the EU/UK opt-in banner and stays the default.
  "No banner (opt-out)" turns everything on, shows a "Your privacy choices" link in the
  footer to switch it off, and turns advertising off for browsers that send Global Privacy
  Control. Switching a shop back to "Ask first" asks those visitors again.
