- **Step-by-step configurator on the product page.** A product's Configurator has an "On the product page" setting:
  all options at once, as before, or step by step. Step by step walks customers through the visible options one at a
  time with a progress bar, keeps their earlier answers above the current step with a link to change each, holds them
  on a required option until it's answered, lets them skip an optional one, and ends on a review before adding to the
  cart. Steps follow the conditions, so an answer that shows or hides a later option changes the count as they go, and
  the add button's "missing option" jump brings that step up.
