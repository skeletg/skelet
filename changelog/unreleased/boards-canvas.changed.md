- **Boards are built in place.** **Add report** on a board offers Insights, Funnel, Flow and
  Retention. Editing a tile opens a panel beside the board while the tile updates as you go, and
  **Expand** shows the report full width. Tiles are small, medium or a full row, reorder by
  dragging (or by keyboard on the handle), duplicate, and save with the board as before. The grid
  is 4 columns wide, or 5 on a very wide screen.
- **Engagement is part of Retention.** One Retention page answers "do people come back?" either as
  a return rate or by cohort. The old Engagement link still opens it, on the return-rate view.
- **A report page keeps the report in its URL.** Insights, Funnel, Flow and Retention take their
  settings and period from the address, so a reload, the back button or a shared link opens the
  same report. Each has a period picker, and on global scope it offers the shops to pick from
  right there.
- **Add to board** opens on the board last added to.
- Charts on these pages draw in the chart colours rather than the brand colour, and retention
  cohorts of fewer than 20 people are faded.
