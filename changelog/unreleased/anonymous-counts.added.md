- **Visitors who decline analytics are counted, as nobody.** Signals still counts their page
  views, cart changes, orders and revenue, without a cookie and without recording anything that
  identifies them.
  - They appear in totals and never as a person, funnel step or unique visitor. Insights says how
    much of a total they make up.
  - The new `general/privacy/aggregate_counts` setting (on by default) turns it off.
  - `docs/privacy.md` explains the legal basis and gives suggested privacy-notice text.
