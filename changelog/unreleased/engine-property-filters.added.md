- **Conditions on every report.** Insights, Funnel steps, Flow, Retention (separately for the start
  and the return) and the return rate take conditions on an event's properties: `=`, `≠` (an event
  without the property passes) and "is one of". The property is picked from a list rather than
  typed, and the value box suggests the property's most common values.
- **Net sales and sums.** Insights can add up a property instead of counting, and "Net sales" in
  its event list is a product's or the shop's sales after discounts and refunds, in the base
  currency.
- **Where did the drop-offs go.** Each funnel step has "See where the 82 went", which opens a Flow
  of the people who did the step before and not this one - on a board, as a tile beside the funnel.
  Flow takes "only people who didn't then…" directly too.
