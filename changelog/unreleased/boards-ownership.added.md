- **Boards are owned and private until shared.** A new board is a draft only its creator sees;
  its first Save keeps it. **Share** gives admins or whole roles view or edit access, and warns
  about anyone who'd see the board without its numbers (no customer analytics on the shop). An
  edit share is enough to change a board, without `boards.manage`.
- **Shop boards** have no owner and everyone on the shop sees them, as all boards did before -
  existing boards and the starter boards are shop boards. An owner can make their board one.
- **Favourites and views on the boards list**: All I can see, Favourites, Mine, Shared with me,
  Shop boards and My drafts, plus Every board for a superuser.
- **Unsaved edits are kept** for 30 days, written as you go, and the board opens on them next
  time with a way to discard them.
- A board whose owner was deleted can be claimed by someone who manages boards on the shop.
