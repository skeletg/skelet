- **The email editor is easier to work in.** Click a block to add it after the selected one, or use the "+"
  between blocks; headings, text and button labels are edited in place; variables are picked by name from a menu
  on each field; the side panel splits into Block, Email and Theme tabs; colours left unset show their default;
  the canvas previews at phone width; and the arrows, Delete, Escape, Ctrl/⌘+D and Ctrl/⌘+Z work on blocks.
- **The email preview shows the email as it lands.** It opens large, with the inbox row (sender, subject,
  preheader), the opened message at desktop width or in a phone, the plain-text part, a dark mode that leaves
  images alone, and checks beside it: subject length, a missing preheader, size against Gmail's clipping limit,
  images without alt text and every link, with the ones that go nowhere flagged.
- **A products block is picked from the catalog.** "Choose products" opens the product picker, the block lists what
  it shows with pictures and names - on the canvas too - and picking more than "Max products" raises it, since a send
  only shows up to that many.
- **The email editor shows how close an email is to being clipped.** A size bar measures the real rendered email
  against Gmail's 102 KB limit and turns amber as it nears it.
- **A new reusable block only needs a name.** Its slug follows the name until typed by hand, and saving without one
  opens Settings with the cursor in the field that's missing.
- **Order blocks can be added to any email**, as they could to a reusable block - an order confirmation built in the
  email editor no longer needs one wrapped in a reusable block.
