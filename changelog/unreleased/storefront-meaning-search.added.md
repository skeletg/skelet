  hikes" - lists the products closest in meaning, the words only helping to order them, and a keyword search that
  matches nothing lists the closest by meaning instead of an empty page, with a line above them saying so. Filters, facets, sorting and paging work as
  on any listing. It needs embeddings.

  or off) and how far it reaches (strict, balanced or broad), and tries a search with the choices before they're saved -
  on the search page, with what the shopper sees and what each half found, and in the assistant, beside it on a tab.

  both are words the catalog doesn't use, even when the shopper got a page of products.
