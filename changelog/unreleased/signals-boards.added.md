- **Boards: shared dashboards of saved reports.** Each shop can keep boards of Insights, Funnel,
  Retention, Engagement and Flow reports side by side, with one period for the whole board.
  Reports are added with **Add to board** on those pages, and edits go through the board's Save,
  which refuses a save over someone else's newer one. Four starter boards (Shop health, Revenue &
  repeat, Product usage, Email impact) can be added to a shop with none. New permissions
  `boards.view` and `boards.manage`.
